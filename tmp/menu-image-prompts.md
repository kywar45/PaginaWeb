Method: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`; new image generation, no reference-image edits. Generated outputs copied into the project without deleting their originals.

## public/img/menu/manicure-promo.png

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: tall vertical photographic background for a luxury nail products ecommerce mega menu, portrait 2:3 aspect ratio.
Primary request: Elegant macro beauty photograph of a woman's hand with very long almond shaped nude pink glossy gel nails, one accent nail with delicate fine silver-pink glitter. Lifelike professionally manicured fingers, graceful pose, fingertips enter from the right and occupy the center-right portion of the canvas. Three to four nails visible at different angles. Soft blush-pink satin backdrop.
Composition/framing: narrow portrait composition with empty soft pink space across upper 20 percent for an HTML brand name, and calmer empty pink lower 25 percent for HTML marketing copy. Hands main focus midway down, some fingers cropped elegantly at right edge. Professional commercial beauty photography, soft diffuse studio light, natural skin texture, bright glossy highlights.
Color palette: dusty blush pink, nude rose, soft cream, warm skin tones.
Constraints: a photograph only, no text, no typography, no logos, no symbols, no badges, no watermark, no border.

## public/img/menu/nail-products.png

Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: ecommerce product catalog sprite sheet, precisely 5 columns and 2 rows of equal square cells, 5:2 wide aspect ratio.
Primary request: Ten isolated realistic nail care product still life photographs in a perfectly regular 5 by 2 grid. Each product or product group centered within its own invisible square cell with generous white margin, all products fully contained in their cell. Entire background pure solid white (#ffffff). Consistent scale and clean soft studio lighting. No visual grid lines or separators.
First row from left to right: (1) small open blush-pink cosmetic gel jar containing rich pink gel with lid behind; (2) single pink nail polish bottle with tall glossy black cap; (3) small black nail acrylic liquid bottle with black cap; (4) small elegant black nail primer bottle, tall slim cap; (5) three professional nail files in pale grey and muted pink, upright fanned arrangement.
Second row from left to right: (1) little open clear pot filled with bright pink glitter flakes; (2) white and light-pink UV LED nail curing lamp, front three-quarter view; (3) transparent rectangular compartment organizer box of nail tips, three-quarter top view; (4) tall pastel pink cylindrical nail cleaner bottle with pink cap; (5) small coordinated group of three pink/black nail polish bottles and two pink gel jars.
Materials: glass, shiny black plastic, pastel pink packaging, transparent acrylic, realistic subtle shadows.
Critical constraints: EXACTLY five equal-width columns and two equal-height rows, equal square cells. Centers horizontally at 10%, 30%, 50%, 70%, 90% and vertically at 25%, 75%. Each still life stays inside center 75 percent of its cell. No printed text whatsoever, no brand names, no typography, no letters, no labels, no watermarks, no extra products between cells, no borders. Pure white seamless background.
